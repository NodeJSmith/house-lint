# HSL104 tuning pass (2026-11-09)

Sets the default `min_tokens` for `HSL104` (D8 in `design.md`), by the method in parent D17: the lowest floor of 30, 40, and 50 tokens whose hand-classified sample of 3+-copy groups is at least two-thirds real duplication.

## Setup

- hassette at `dcaeea40` (2026-11-09). Python files under `client/`, `src/`, `tests/`, and `wire/`, with `__pycache__`, `node_modules`, and `snapshots` pruned: 1,104 files. The 8 carrying hassette's "Do not edit this file directly" banner were excluded, leaving 1,096.
- jscpd 5.4.1 via `uvx --from jscpd==5.4.1`, given the file list through `-c`, with `--no-gitignore --absolute --mode weak --min-tokens K`. Default `--min-lines` (5). No gap merging and no normalization.
- Grouping: `design/research/2026-10-18-duplicate-code-detection/probe/scripts/clusters.py` (D1: merge overlapping fragments within a file, then group pairs transitively), keeping groups of 3+ copies.
- Sample: 15 groups per floor, drawn with `random.Random(39)`. The 42 unique groups were pooled and shuffled, so the classifier never saw which floor a group came from. One agent classified every group as `real` (a fixture, `parametrize`, or helper would remove it), `boilerplate` (deliberate parallel API, overloads, per-method docs, import lists), or `signature` (parameter lists with no shared body).

## Result

| Floor | Pairs | Groups (2+) | Groups (3+) | Sample real | Boilerplate | Signature | Real share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 1,193 | 653 | 150 | 12/15 | 2 | 1 | 80% |
| 40 | 582 | 352 | 64 | 6/15 | 7 | 2 | 40% |
| 50 | 299 | 184 | 28 | 6/15 | 6 | 3 | 40% |

30 is the default. It is the only floor that clears two-thirds, and by a margin (10 of 15 would pass). Lower floors are cleaner because the short groups are mostly repeated test setup. The large groups that survive at 40 and 50 are dominated by deliberate repetition: hassette's async/sync entity wrappers, per-method `Args:` docstring paragraphs on the Bus and Scheduler APIs, `@overload` stubs, and import lists.

## Chaining

D1 merges any overlapping fragments within a file, even by one line, so a thin overlap can join clusters. Measured on the 30-token report by regrouping with a stricter rule, which merges only overlaps of at least 5 lines (`min_lines`):

| Merge rule | Within-file merges | Merges overlapping < 5 lines | Groups of 3+ | Largest group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Any overlap (D1) | 483 | 230 | 150 | 34 |
| Overlap ≥ 5 lines | 253 | 0 | 137 | 18 |

Under the stricter rule, 65 of the 150 groups split, and 29 of them drop below 3 copies. In the 30-token sample:
- G01, G10, and G18, all classified real, split into pairs and stop reporting.
- G20, G26, and G35 (real) and G39 (boilerplate) split into smaller groups that still report.
- No group classified boilerplate or signature disappears.

The thin overlaps here mostly join copied tests with small variations, where each copy partially matches the next, so the chain is the duplication. D1's any-overlap merge stays.

## Line floor

jscpd's `--min-lines`, measured at 30 tokens. hassette had moved to `d6aa2f01` by this run, so the 5-line row (1,181 pairs, 654 groups) differs slightly from the Result table's 30-token row, which was measured at `dcaeea40`. jscpd itself is deterministic: two identical reruns at `d6aa2f01` both gave 1,181 pairs. The percentages in `design.md` D4 compare rows from this table only.

| `min_lines` | Pairs | Groups (2+) | Groups of 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,781 | 905 | 116 |
| 3 | 1,615 | 835 | 104 |
| 5 (default) | 1,181 | 654 | 75 |
| 8 | 491 | 309 | 36 |
| 10 | 245 | 167 | 15 |

The line floor shapes the output as much as the token floor does. Without it, about 600 more pairs come in, all 2-4-line fragments of dense code. jscpd's floor looks exclusive: at `min_lines` 5, the shortest reported fragment is 6 lines. The build checks this, and the docs describe the option by what jscpd actually does.
